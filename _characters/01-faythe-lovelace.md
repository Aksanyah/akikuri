Once mocked as "basic" by per peers, Faythe is mortally wounded while spontaneously embarking on a dangerous missionary trip. But when she miraculously wakes up in a future, preserved in a pumpkin spice latte healing vat, she gains the powers of infinite empathy and elite BASIC mastery, giving her the potential to hack the fabric of reality. Can she use her new skills to rally the denizens of future Earth, the Heap, with her trademark blend of coziness and faith?
