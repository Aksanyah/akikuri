Faythe Lovelace was a girl who loved the simple pleasures in life: a pile of
crunchy leaves in an apple orchard, the smell of cloves and nutmeg from a hot
beverage, the fluffy softness of a knit wool sweater. And, of course, Jesus.

Still, it was a shock when Faythe hastily agreed to a special overseas mission
trip: smuggling digital Bibles past a hostile nation's border. Although a customs
checkpoint misunderstanding sends her into a coma, her mind continues to function
in a perpetual loop of decoding Bible patterns backward and forward.

Many years later, Faythe awakes in a future dystopian world called the Heap,
preserved in a healing tank of java and aromatic spices. A covert hacker team,
Kakure, reveals that her ordeal has bestowed her with infinite empathy and an
elite programming skill: BASIC mastery. The autumn-loving Christian girl once
mocked by her peers as "basic" can now hack the fabric of reality in the
unlikeliest of ways!

With the help of Kakure, Faythe ventures off to the Stacks (a network of
low-orbit arcologies) to defeat their high-tech, demonic megacorps. Can the
Heap's newest Pumpkin Spice Cyberpunker replace corruption with coziness and
bring salvation to the hopeless?
